# ASTRA: 30-second spot, "The Date"

Comedy. Deadpan narrator; the jokes are in the picture and in ASTRA's real screen text. Every line on the FM-1's
screen below is one the firmware can actually print (a transit reading, a house line, Saturn's title from Crowley's
*777*). Drafted with the local model (qwen3.5:9b) over three rounds; final punch-up by the director.

## Stingers

Picked:

1. **Text the goth back. Or Saturn.** (used above)
2. Impress the goth baddie. Or impress the Demiurge.
3. Date the brooding type. Or ascend past the Archons.

Also in the running: "Get past their friends. Or get past the seven spheres." · "Look cool at the table. Or hear the planets
argue about your love life."

The director's original, for the record: "Wanna convince that goth baddy you're with it? Or ascend the spheres like a
gnostic hero of old."

## Facts the spot relies on (all true of the firmware)

- The planets are computed on the device, offline. The aspects of your birth chart wire the FM engine.
- "DISCIPLINE PULLS AGAINST LOVE" is a transit reading (Saturn opposite your Venus); "FROM PARTNERS TO SECLUSION" a
  house line; "THE GREAT ONE OF THE NIGHT OF TIME" is Saturn's trump title in *777* (1909), on the BIRTH CHART page.
- WARP runs the sky up to five years a second. An opposition sounds as a loop.
- ASTRA reads one chart against the sky. It does not compare two people's charts; the spot never says it does.
- Not shown, deliberately: any claim that the readings predict anything.
