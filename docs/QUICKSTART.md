# Start here: ASTRA in five minutes

ASTRA turns your FM-1 into an instrument played by the sky. It knows where the planets are, it learns your birth chart, and the angles between the planets decide the sound. One warning first: the buttons still wear their factory names, but most do something else in ASTRA. Here are the ones you need.

## The only buttons you need

| Button | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **HOME** | The wheel: the main page, your planets and the lines between them |
| **SEQ** | Your horoscope: today's sky against your birth chart |
| **GLO** | The clock and your birth details (press again for the next page) |
| **PLAY** | On the clock page: "this time is right". Elsewhere: pause or run the sky |
| **REC** | Back to now. On a birth page: make a sound from your chart |
| **SAVE** | Press twice to keep the sound you're playing |

**The knobs:** the row of four does whatever the four labels at the bottom of the screen say. ALGORITHM changes how the planets are wired together, PRESETS picks one of 64 sounds, and SELECT picks a planet (or sets minutes).

## Your first five minutes

1. Switch on and play a few keys.
2. Turn ALGORITHM and listen: the lines on the wheel and the sound change together.
3. Press GLO to set the clock. Use the four knobs for year, month, day, and hour; use SELECT for minutes. Press PLAY. The top bar says CLOCK SET.
4. Press GLO again to enter your birth date and time. Press GLO once more for your birth place: latitude, longitude, time zone. (No birth time? Set TIME to unknown.) Then press REC: the sound you hear now is your chart.
5. Press HOME to return to the wheel. Turn ALGORITHM left until the top bar says TRANSIT H1. You are now hearing today's sky playing against your personal chart.
6. Press SEQ to read your transits. Turn SELECT to read the next one.

## What you are hearing

Each planet creates a tone; when two planets form an angle, one bends the other. This bending makes the sound change as the real sky moves.

- **Conjunction** (together): a strong bend
- **Trine**: a smooth bend
- **Sextile**: a light touch
- **Square**: a metallic ring
- **Opposition**: the two bend each other, a loop

The closer the angle is to exact, the stronger the bend.

## Reading a transit

Each transit is read in four lines. For example:

- **MOON CONJUNCT URANUS**: what is happening
- **FEELING MERGES WITH CHANGE**: what it means
- **FROM VOCATION TO VOCATION**: where in your life (here, your career)
- **SEPARATING 0°13' EXACT TODAY**: it peaked today and is fading

These are symbols to reflect on, not predictions.

## Three good things to know

- **Your birth chart is remembered** when you switch off. Sounds are kept only when you press SAVE twice.
- **Make the sky race:** press LFO and turn WARP to the right, up to five years a second. To come back, turn WARP to the centre and press REC.
- **Four notes at once** on the 27 keys, or over MIDI.
